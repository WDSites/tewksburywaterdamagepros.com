How to Choose Black Mold Removal Companies in Tewksbury, MA

A buyer guide for picking a mold contractor, with questions you can ask on the first call.

By the Tewksbury Water Damage Pros team · 2026

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

Choosing among black mold removal companies in Tewksbury, MA is easier when you know what a good one puts on paper. Look for a written scope, a containment plan, a stated drying method, independent clearance and a plan to fix the moisture. Names and ads tell you much less.

This is a decision guide, not a ranking. We do not list competitors. Instead, you get a checklist for any company you call, the warning signs that should end the conversation, and the questions that sort careful crews from the rest.

What "black mold" means and what it does not

Black mold is a popular label. Color alone does not identify a mold, since growth on drywall, wood and grout can look dark gray, green or black depending on the species and the surface. Only a trained assessment settles what you have, and the Massachusetts guide does not recommend routine testing for visible mold anyway.

So the right question to ask a company is not whether it removes black mold. It is how it removes any mold: containment, removal, cleaning, drying and a fix for the moisture. Health questions belong with a physician.

Red flags that should end the call

  • No written scope, or a scope that is one line long.
  • Pressure to sign today because the mold is "spreading fast."
  • An offer to skip fixing the moisture source to save money.
  • A demand for full payment before any work starts.
  • A promise that the mold will never come back. No honest contractor can say that.
  • Health claims or diagnoses from the person selling the job.

Questions to ask on the phone

  1. Will someone walk the affected area and write a scope before work begins?
  2. Do your crews work to ANSI/IICRC S520, and can you describe how?
  3. How will you contain the work area and filter the air?
  4. What happens to the moisture source, and who fixes it?
  5. Will an independent party check the work afterward?
  6. Can I have references and see how you handle payment installments?

Listen for specifics. A company that names HEPA air scrubbers, moisture meters and a drying plan is thinking about your house. A company that talks only about its speed is selling.

Safety note

People with asthma or weakened immune systems should not do mold cleanup themselves, per the Massachusetts DPH guide. Hidden mold is another reason to bring in professionals.

No state licence means you do the vetting

Massachusetts does not require a licence for mold remediation. That puts the burden on you, and the DPH guide says to check experience, references and complaint history. Compare at least two written scopes and ask whether the price includes rebuild or only removal.

Our mold remediation service works to the S520 standard with licensed and insured restoration professionals. If the mold grew because of a leak, water damage cleanup may need to come first so the structure is dry before the mold work starts.
