How coverage works

Coverage means dispatch will send a crew to your address when one is free. We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic on Interstate 93, Interstate 495 and Route 3 and other emergencies change it. Tell the dispatcher your cross street and we confirm coverage on the call.

Rivers define the map here. The Merrimack runs along the northern edge of Tewksbury and through Lowell, the Shawsheen runs through southern Tewksbury and past Wilmington and Andover, and the Concord River separates Tewksbury from Chelmsford. Water that comes from a river or from heavy rain is flood damage, and flood damage restoration explains how that is handled.

Before the crew arrives

While you wait, make the space easier to work in. Move small valuables, rugs and electronics out of the wet area if the floor is safe to walk on, and keep children and pets away from standing water. If the water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, stay out of the room and keep the power off until a qualified person says it is safe.

Take photos or short video of the damage and the source before anything is moved, because your insurance carrier may ask for them. Keep any damaged items that you can, and write down what was affected and when you first noticed it.

What to tell dispatch

  • Your street address and cross street or a nearby landmark
  • Where the water came from and whether it is still arriving
  • Whether it is clean water, drain water or flood water
  • Which rooms and floors are affected
  • Whether you have already shut off the main valve or power
