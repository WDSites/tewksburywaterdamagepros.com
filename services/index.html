Which service do I need?

Water damage rarely shows up as one clean problem. A burst pipe becomes standing water, then wet drywall, then a musty closet. We organized the services by the stage you are in, so you can start at the page that matches what you see.

If water is still moving, call first and read after. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, and the pages below explain what each stage involves and how scope and price are set.

Water is still arriving or standing

Start with emergency water removal, then drying through water mitigation.

Water stopped but things are soaked

Cleanup removes what cannot be saved. See water damage cleanup for the sorting rules.

The room is dry and needs rebuilding

Water damage repair covers walls, ceilings, floors and paint.

Water came from outside or a drain

Flood damage restoration treats it as contaminated water.

All six services

Water Damage Repair

Rebuilding after drying: drywall, plaster ceilings, hardwood and finishes. If the room is still wet, water mitigation comes first.

Emergency Water Mitigation

The first 24 to 72 hours: moisture mapping, extraction and structural drying so the structure does not keep absorbing water.

Emergency Water Damage Cleanup

Removing soaked and contaminated materials, sanitizing and deodorizing, with a written list of what was removed.

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extraction units for standing water, burst pipes, appliance failures and flooded levels.

Emergency Flood Damage Restoration

Basement flooding and sewage backup, handled as contaminated water under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

Mold Remediation

Source-first mold removal after leaks, with containment and answers about testing and clearance for a sale or loan.
