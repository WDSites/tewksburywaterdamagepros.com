Mold Cleanup Cost in Tewksbury, MA: What Sets the Price

The honest answer is that scope sets the price. Here is how to read a scope and ask the right questions.

By the Tewksbury Water Damage Pros team · 2026

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

How much does mold cleanup cost in Tewksbury, MA? It depends on how much is affected, what it is growing on, and what has to be rebuilt afterward. A number quoted over the phone for a basement or attic you have not shown anyone cannot be exact, and anyone who says otherwise is selling.

What you can get is a written scope that lists the work, so that two companies can be compared on the same terms. This page walks through the factors that raise or lower a quote and what that paper should say.

What changes the number

  • Size of the affected area, including what a moisture meter finds beyond the visible patch.
  • Material type: hard surfaces clean up, while drywall, carpet and insulation are usually removed.
  • Containment and negative air: plastic barriers, HEPA air scrubbers and the number of days they run.
  • Water damage that must be repaired too, such as wet framing, subfloor or insulation.
  • Hidden spread inside walls or above a ceiling, which only shows once material is opened.
  • Testing and clearance, if a lender, buyer or your own plan calls for an independent check.
  • Rebuild after removal: new drywall, paint, trim and flooring.
  • Access: a tight crawl space or a finished basement slows the crew down.

Notice that the mold itself is only part of the list. A good share of the work in many jobs is the water damage around it and the rebuild after it, which is why a price for cleanup alone can be misleading.

The honest answer about a number on the phone

A phone number is a range at best. Mold on the surface of a bathroom ceiling might be the whole story, or the ceiling might sit over a wet cavity that runs another eight feet. Neither is visible from a description.

A technician who walks the area with a moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera can mark what is wet and what is only stained. That visit produces the scope, and the scope produces the price. If the number comes first, be careful.

What a written scope should list

  1. The moisture source

    The scope should name how the leak or condensation will be fixed, or who is responsible for fixing it.

  2. Area and materials

    Rooms, square footage, and which materials are removed and which are cleaned.

  3. Containment plan

    Barriers, HEPA filtration and negative air, with the equipment named.

  4. Removal and cleaning method

    How porous material is bagged and how hard surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and cleaned.

  5. Drying and clearance

    Drying equipment, how you will know the area is dry, and whether independent clearance is included or separate.

  6. Rebuild

    Itemized and priced separately, so you can approve it or hire someone else.

Vetting a company when there is no state licence

Massachusetts does not require a licence for mold remediation. The DPH homeowner guide tells you to check experience, references and complaint history instead. Compare at least two written scopes, ask each company whether crews work to ANSI/IICRC S520, and ask for references you can actually call.

On payment, the guide says to pay in installments and hold the final payment until the work is checked. A company that wants the full amount before starting is asking you to carry all the risk.

Local note

What Tewksbury homeowners should know

The Shawsheen River runs through the southern end of Tewksbury, in one of the oldest sections of town. Older houses often have plaster, older paint and cellars that have been wet before. Disturbing more than 6 square feet of painted interior surface triggers lead-safe renovation rules under the state guide, so tell the company the age of the house when you ask for a scope.

If you also own or manage property in Wilmington or Chelmsford, the same scope questions apply there. Ask each company what it has done in jobs like yours.
