Emergency Water Damage Cleanup in Tewksbury, MA

We pull out soaked materials, clean and sanitize what stays, and leave the home ready for drying and repair.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

Water damage cleanup in Tewksbury, MA is the work of removing what the water ruined and cleaning what it touched. The first 48 hours matter most, so our 24/7 dispatch line is open at night and on weekends. While you wait, stop the source, keep everyone out of the wet room and lift valuables off the floor. Cleanup is judged by the water category, and ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard that defines those categories.

Cleanup sits between water mitigation, which stops the spread and dries the structure, and water damage repair, which rebuilds. It answers a narrow question: what has to leave the house, and what can be saved by cleaning.

What gets removed and what gets cleaned

Not every wet item has to go. The category of water and how long the material stayed wet decide what is salvageable.

MaterialClean water (Category 1)Contaminated water (Category 2 or 3)
Carpet padOften removedRemoved
CarpetCan often be dried or cleanedUsually removed
Drywall and insulationDried or cut out depending on exposureCut out and discarded
Hard surfaces and tileCleaned and driedCleaned and sanitized
Upholstery and fabricsCase by caseUsually discarded

Category 1 water comes from a clean source such as a supply line. Category 2 carries significant contamination, as from a washing machine or dishwasher discharge. Category 3 is grossly contaminated, as with sewage or river flood water. Clean water can turn into Category 2 as it sits and absorbs what it touches.

Cleanup in the first 48 hours

Cleanup that starts quickly keeps more of your belongings. Soaked carpet pad holds water against the subfloor, and wet drywall begins to break down. Taking these out early gives the structure a chance to dry before mold has a foothold.

The Massachusetts Department of Public Health guidance on mold says wet areas should be dried within 24 hours, and the state housing code allows 48 hours for areas exposed to leaks or floods. Cleanup is where those targets are met or missed.

Contaminated water

If water came from a sewer or drain backup, or from flood water, stay out of it. Do not wade through it, run a vacuum in it or touch wet materials without protection. Call the Town water and sewer office about a municipal line problem, call 911 about any electrical or gas risk, then call us.

Sanitizing, deodorizing and contents

After materials are removed, surfaces are cleaned with detergent and treated with an antimicrobial where the water category calls for it. Odor often points to moisture left in a cavity, so we check readings before masking a smell.

Contents such as furniture, boxes and electronics are sorted into salvageable and not. We photograph and list items before disposal so you have documentation for your insurer.

What affects the price of cleanup

Cleanup pricing follows the amount of removal and the contamination level. A clean-water leak in one room is a different job from a gray-water loss across a finished basement.

  • Water category and exposure time
  • Area affected and number of rooms
  • Materials removed and disposal volume
  • Contents handling and pack-out
  • Sanitizing or antimicrobial treatment
