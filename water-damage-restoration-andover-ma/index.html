Old houses, old plumbing

Nobody can say what is inside a wall in a house like the 1673 Chandler-Bigsby-Abbot House, and that is the lesson for any older Andover home. Supply lines, drain stacks and radiator pipes from different decades can meet in one wall. When one fails, the water travels along framing and shows up in a ceiling two rooms away.

Plaster and lath hold water differently from drywall. Plaster can swell, crack and release from its lath, so a stain on an old ceiling often means opening a section to see how far the damp has gone. Our water damage repair crews map moisture first, then decide what to cut.

When water is spreading in Andover

A ceiling that sags, a tub overflowing upstairs or a failed washing machine hose will not wait for morning. Dispatch for Andover runs 24/7, and the call comes before the cleanup. Shut off the water at the main if you can find it, and put a bucket under any bulge.

Do not poke a sagging ceiling to drain it unless you can stand clear. Water trapped in plaster is heavy, and a section can drop all at once.

Drying plaster, ceilings and framing

Mitigation means getting the structure dry before it rots or grows mold. Crews set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, check readings with moisture meters and use a thermal imaging camera to trace damp behind finished surfaces. Water mitigation usually takes several days, and the equipment readings tell the crew when to stop.

If the paint on affected walls is old, disturbing more than 6 square feet of it triggers lead-safe renovation rules in Massachusetts. Ask any contractor how they handle that before demolition begins.

The town reservoir, Haggetts Pond, and its 1,442-acre watershed supply your tap, with Fish Brook and the Merrimack River supplementing, and the treatment plant is at 397 Lowell Street. If water is still spreading in Andover

Shut off the water

Find the main valve before the ceiling decides for you. In an older house it may be a round wheel handle near the cellar stairs.

Panel check

If the electrical panel is dry and within easy reach, switch off breakers over and under the wet area. Otherwise leave it alone and call the utility or 911, since wet plaster and wiring are a bad mix.

Clear the floor

Antiques, books and rugs go to a dry room. Heavy furniture can stay put if you slide foil or plastic under the legs.

Stay out from under

Do not stand beneath a bulging ceiling, and keep pets and kids out of that room until a crew has looked at it.

Phone the 24/7 line

Say which floor is leaking, whether the water is still flowing and what age the house is.
