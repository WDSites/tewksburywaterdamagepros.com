Emergency Flood Damage Restoration in Tewksbury, MA

Basement flooding, river water and sewage backup need fast, careful cleanup. Dispatch is open 24/7.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

Flood damage restoration in Tewksbury, MA covers water that comes from outside the plumbing: heavy rain, groundwater, a failed sump pump or a backed-up sewer line. It is an emergency, and our dispatch line is open 24/7. Stay out of the water, keep children and pets away and avoid touching anything electrical until the power situation is safe.

Flood water is treated as contaminated, which changes how everything is cleaned and what can be saved. The work combines emergency water removal, water damage cleanup and drying, with stricter handling of materials the water touched.

Why flood water is different

Under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, flood water and sewage are Category 3, which means grossly contaminated. Porous materials such as carpet, pad and drywall that soak in it are usually removed instead of dried.

Hard surfaces can be cleaned and sanitized. That is why flood jobs involve more demolition than a clean supply line leak, and why a written scope matters before anything comes out.

Stay out of flood and sewage water

Do not wade through it, do not run a vacuum in it and do not use electrical tools near it. If water reached the panel or outlets, call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

Basement flooding cleanup

Basements flood from rain that overwhelms drainage, groundwater rising through the slab, sump pump failure or a sewer backup. The cause decides the water category and the cleanup.

We pump the water out, remove soaked finishes, clean and sanitize the floor and walls, and set drying equipment. Boxes, furniture and stored items are sorted into salvageable and not. After heavy rain, a finished basement can hide wet drywall and insulation for days, so moisture readings come before any rebuild.

Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps coming in after the cleanup, a waterproofing or drainage contractor fixes the way in, and we can work around that schedule.

Sewage backup cleanup

Sewage backup from a floor drain or toilet is Category 3 water. Tewksbury wastewater leaves town through interceptors to the Lowell Regional Wastewater Utility, so a backup can come from your own lateral or from the municipal side. Call the Town water and sewer office if neighbors are affected.

Crews wear protective equipment, remove porous materials, clean and sanitize the hard surfaces and dry the space. Do not try to clean sewage yourself.

What the region has seen

In March 2010, the National Weather Service recorded 7 to 10 inches of rain from Methuen and Gloucester southward in a few days. The Shawsheen River at Wilmington reached a record 10.59 feet, and the Merrimack at Lowell reached 53.63 feet. The report does not name Tewksbury, but it shows how fast the rivers around us can rise.

The practical point is the same for any address: check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your property, and ask your insurer about flood coverage before you need it.

Insurance and flood coverage

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance says standard homeowners policies do not cover flood damage. Some policies add an endorsement for sump pump failure or water backup, so check your declarations page.

FEMA notes that National Flood Insurance Program policies typically take effect 30 days after purchase. We do not promise coverage, and we provide a written scope and photos for whatever claim you file.
