Mold after a leak

Mold needs moisture. The Massachusetts mold guide says to find the water source and fix it, because mold will not stop growing otherwise. Wet areas should be dried in 24 hours, and anything over 10 square feet is a job for professionals. Mold remediation follows the S520 standard, and Massachusetts has no license for it, so check references.

If water is still spreading in Chelmsford

Find your valve

Know which district bills you, then locate the shutoff inside the house. Closing it stops a supply-line leak, and the district only needs a call if the street side fails.

Panel rules

Cut breakers to wet rooms only when the panel is dry and clear of standing water. Otherwise call the utility or 911 about the electrical hazard before anything else.

Get things off the floor

Rugs, boxes and low furniture move to dry ground. A cellar in an older Chelmsford house may have a stone or poured floor that stays wet longer.

Protect people

Drain or toilet water is unsafe to wade through, so children and pets stay on a dry level.
